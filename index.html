SECTION 01

SCIENTIFIC OVERVIEW & COLLISION THEORY

Experimental Purpose

"Determine the influence of Concentration, Temperature, Surface Area, and Catalyst on the Rate of Reaction in a chemical reaction."

Theoretical Foundation

"The rate of reaction is the change in concentration of reactants or products per unit of time. According to collision theory, chemical reactions occur when reactant particles collide effectively with sufficient energy ($\ge E_a$) and proper spatial orientation."

Rate Equation Rate = -Δ[Reactant]/Δt = +Δ[Product]/Δt

SECTION 05

SCIENTIFIC DISCUSSION & MECHANISTIC RATIONALE

CONCENTRATION MECHANISM

Particle Density Factor

Higher reactant concentration increases the number of reactive particles per unit volume. This higher density increases the statistical frequency of particle collisions per second.

Direct Result: 3M HCl reaction completed 7s faster than 1M HCl.
TEMPERATURE MECHANISM

Maxwell-Boltzmann Shift

Elevating thermal energy increases average kinetic energy ($E_k = \frac{3}{2}kT$). A larger fraction of colliding particles possess kinetic energy exceeding the activation energy ($E \ge E_a$).

Direct Result: 10°C rise reduced duration from 75s to 50s.
SURFACE AREA MECHANISM

Exposed Reactive Sites

Crushing the solid eggshell ($CaCO_3$) increases total exposed surface area, allowing more H⁺ ions in solution to interact simultaneously with the solid matrix.

Direct Result: Powdered sample reacted in 65s vs 240s uncrushed.
CATALYST MECHANISM

Transition State Pathway

Fe³⁺ provides an alternative multi-step reaction pathway with lower activation energy ($E_a$). Na⁺ from NaCl displays no such catalytic activity in peroxide decomposition.

Direct Result: Rapid gas evolution, steam formation, and amber intermediate.
SECTION 06

FINAL CONCLUSION & CORE FINDINGS

01. CONCENTRATION

Higher concentration increases collision frequency, accelerating reaction velocity.

02. TEMPERATURE

Higher temperature increases kinetic energy and the fraction of particles with $E \ge E_a$.

03. SURFACE AREA

Greater exposed surface area increases accessible active collision sites.

04. CATALYST

A suitable catalyst lowers activation energy ($E_a$) via an alternative reaction route.

CENTRAL SCIENTIFIC TAKEAWAY

"REACTION RATE IS GOVERNED BY THE FREQUENCY AND EFFECTIVENESS OF PARTICLE COLLISIONS."
